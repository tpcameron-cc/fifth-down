Fifth Down

Name the formation in five snaps

    How to play

    A secret formation is hidden on the grid. It could be an offense or a defense. You get 5 guesses to name it.

    • Type a formation and press Snap. It gets drawn on the grid: O for offense, X for defense.
    • Any player standing in the same square as the same kind of player in the answer lights up and stays lit.
    • Players in the wrong spot stay on the grid faded, from every guess so far, so you can see which squares are ruled out.
    • Guess 4-3 when the answer is 4-4 and the four down linemen light up, plus any corners or safeties in the same spots.
